# WebAthens

## The Digital Agora for Trust, Interoperability, and Human Agency

### Mission

Advance trusted interoperability across people, organizations, AI systems, and connected devices.

### Vision

A world where trust, interoperability, accountability, privacy, recognition, and human agency are foundational to digital collaboration.

WebAthens is a collaborative initiative exploring how trust, interoperability, accountability, privacy, and human agency can help people, organizations, AI systems, and connected devices work together more effectively.

Inspired by the ancient Agora of Athens, WebAthens serves as a modern gathering place where innovators, researchers, startups, nonprofits, standards organizations, investors, and community leaders can exchange ideas, build partnerships, and explore responsible innovation.

## Current Status

WebAthens is an early-stage initiative.

We are currently focused on defining principles, building community, exploring partnerships, and identifying opportunities for collaboration.

## Areas of Exploration

- Responsible & Accountable AI
- Trust Infrastructure
- Interoperability & Open Standards
- Digital Identity
- Verifiable Credentials
- Privacy & Selective Sharing
- Recognition & Reputation Systems
- Community-Led Innovation
- Collective Intelligence

## Founding Principles

1. Trust by Design
2. Privacy by Default
3. Interoperability First
4. Human Agency Matters
5. Accountability Builds Trust
6. Recognition Encourages Contribution
7. Collaboration Accelerates Innovation

## Open Collaboration

WebAthens is being built through open collaboration.

We welcome ideas, discussions, feedback, proposals, and future contributions from those interested in building a more trusted, interoperable, and human-centered future.

## Stewardship

WebAthens is an independent initiative.

Participation is open, and stewardship is intended to evolve alongside the community as WebAthens grows.
