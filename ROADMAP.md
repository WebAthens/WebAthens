# Roadmap

WebAthens is being built as a long-term collaborative initiative exploring trust, interoperability, accountability, privacy, and human agency.

---

# Phase 1: Establish the Digital Agora

## Foundation

- Define mission, vision, and principles
- Publish manifesto
- Launch WebAthens.com
- Establish collaboration channels
- Document governance and contribution guidelines

### Success

- Clear identity
- Open participation model
- Initial collaborators

---

# Phase 2: Community Formation

## Build Relationships

- Connect with researchers, startups, nonprofits, and standards organizations
- Encourage community discussions
- Share resources and research
- Identify collaboration opportunities

### Success

- Active participation
- Growing network
- Emerging initiatives

---

# Phase 3: Collaborative Initiatives

## Move from Discussion to Action

Support initiatives focused on:

- Responsible AI
- Trust Infrastructure
- Digital Identity
- Verifiable Credentials
- Privacy & Selective Sharing
- Interoperability

### Success

- Pilot projects
- Research collaborations
- Real-world experimentation

---

# Phase 4: Trusted Interoperability Ecosystem

## Connect Communities and Organizations

Explore frameworks and approaches that strengthen interoperability across:

- People
- Organizations
- Communities
- AI Systems
- Connected Devices

### Success

- Cross-sector collaboration
- Shared frameworks
- Greater interoperability

---

# Phase 5: Digital Agora Ecosystem

## Long-Term Vision

Create a thriving Digital Agora where people, organizations, AI systems, and connected devices collaborate through trusted interoperability.

The goal is to enable partnerships, initiatives, standards efforts, pilot projects, and innovations that may not otherwise emerge.

### Success

- Trusted collaboration networks
- Community-led initiatives
- Responsible innovation ecosystems
- Sustainable participation

---

# Guiding Principle

WebAthens is not pursuing a predetermined destination.

It seeks to create the conditions where the most impactful ideas, collaborations, initiatives, and innovations can emerge.
