# WebAthens Founding Principles

## 1. Trust by Design

Trust should be intentionally designed into systems, communities, and technologies.

## 2. Privacy by Default

People and organizations should maintain meaningful control over their information.

## 3. Interoperability First

Innovation flourishes when people, organizations, systems, and communities can work together.

## 4. Human Agency Matters

Technology should expand human capability, not diminish it.

## 5. Accountability Builds Trust

Trust grows when actions, decisions, and contributions can be understood, verified, and evaluated.

## 6. Recognition Encourages Contribution

Meaningful contributions deserve meaningful recognition.

## 7. Collaboration Accelerates Innovation

Many of the world's most important challenges cannot be solved alone.

## 8. Communities Help Shape the Future

Communities should have meaningful opportunities to influence how innovations are developed, adopted, and governed.

## 9. Responsible Innovation Requires Stewardship

The most durable innovations balance progress with thoughtful stewardship.

## 10. Build for the Long Term

Sustainable ecosystems are built through patience, integrity, transparency, and continuous learning.
